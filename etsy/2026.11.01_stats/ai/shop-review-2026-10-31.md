*Written 31 Oct 2026 by Claude from the public shop page, the all-time Shop Stats page (PDF in the parent folder) and the Etsy Ads CSV export (parent folder). Conversation: claude.ai, 31 Oct 2026.*

**Bottom line:** the product and the brand are the strong part. The numbers say the shop has a **conversion problem first and a visibility problem second**, and Etsy Ads is currently paying to send people to listings that don't close. 3,455 visits in two years turned into 13 orders (0.4%); a typical Etsy shop converts roughly 1–3%. Organic Etsy search has sent only 195 visits in two years. Fix the listings, then the ads start working and search starts picking you up.

## 1. Why visitors don't buy

Interest is clearly there — 177 different people favorited items, 15 put something in the cart and left, the tweed sweater alone has 49 favorites and 6 people with it in their cart. Something stops them at the last step.

- **Photos.** Most primary images are folded stacks or flat close-ups of texture. The baby alpaca shawl scarf's thumbnail is four folded textiles — a buyer can't tell it's a scarf, how big it is, or how it drapes. For a EUR 100–150 garment bought sight-unseen from abroad, the first photo has to be on a person. The tweed sweater and the lambswool vest are the two best-presented listings and the two most-viewed.
- **Shipping and returns are vague where it counts.** Shop policies say "see item details" for both delivery times and returns; the policy section was last updated Nov 2024. A buyer deciding on a $160 sweater shipped from Kyiv wants, in the listing itself: processing time, typical delivery to US/EU/UK, "tracked, duties prepaid for US," and a clear return window. The FAQ already has most of this — it isn't where the decision happens. Reviews repeatedly praise fast shipping and packaging; that belongs in the first two lines of every description.
- **Free shipping is inconsistent.** 12 listings show "FREE shipping," the 6 scarves under $100 don't — while the FAQ says delivery is included in the price. US search gives a visible bump to free-shipping listings.
- **Sizes.** Sweaters need a size dropdown (variations), not only measurements in the description. (Later check of the listing pages: most garments do have size variations, but many sizes show as sold out — see the listing doc.)
- **One-offs go inactive and take their history with them.** "Royal alpaca handcrafted fine scarf" had 338 views and 10 favorites and is inactive; the merino top from an October review is no longer on the shop page. Since custom colours and sizes are offered anyway, list pieces as made-to-order with quantity > 1 so a sale doesn't kill the listing, its favorites, or its review.

## 2. Listings and search visibility

195 search visits in two years means Etsy's search essentially doesn't know what the shop sells.

- **Lead titles with what people type.** Etsy weighs the first words heavily. "Herringbone light royal alpaca throw scarf, artisanal luxury winter gift" → start with *Alpaca scarf*. "Throw scarf" isn't a search term; "artisanal luxury" isn't either.
- **Say who it's for.** None of the 18 titles say women's, men's or unisex. "Mens wool sweater" and "alpaca scarf women" are large queries.
- **Two listings share the exact same title** ("Baby alpaca batwing sweater with cowl neck, soft fair isle two-tone knit") and compete with each other.
- **Section name:** "wool scarfs and throws" → "Scarves & wraps."
- **Tags and attributes:** use all 13 tags per listing with long-tail phrases, and fill every attribute (colour, material, occasion, recipient).
- **18 listings is thin.** Target 40–60: each colourway of the tweed sweater and the shawl scarf as its own listing, plus a few entry-price items (beanie, cowl, headband, wrist warmers at $35–60). A $45 first purchase is how the shop gets to 20 reviews and Star Seller.

The full rewrite of all 18 titles, tags and descriptions is in `listing-titles-tags-descriptions.md`.

The only month ads clearly paid was **Nov 2025** ($103 → $367, 1.16% conversion at ~$3.50/day). Everything outside Nov–Feb has been close to zero return, and the biggest spend ever — Sep–Oct 2026 at $7–8.50/day — produced 3 orders for $359.

- When the daily budget went up (Sep 2026, and $25/day in Mar 2026), conversion dropped rather than rose. Etsy spends extra budget on broader, lower-intent placements. More budget is not the lever.
- Spending Mar–Aug on winter knitwear ($160 total, 0 orders) was wasted. Demand is Nov–Jan.

**Recommendation:** $3–5/day for November–January only; restrict to the listings that have ever converted (tweed sweater, baby alpaca shawl scarf, brushed alpaca-mohair scarf) plus the best-photographed new ones; switch off any listing with 50+ ad clicks and zero orders; check weekly. At $0.33 a click, 1% conversion means ~$33 per order, which fits inside the margin on a $110 order after Etsy's ~10% fees. At today's 0.3% it's $100–145 per order. Don't scale spend until listing fixes push conversion past 1%.

## 4. Housekeeping on the shop page

- The About video shows "trouble loading this video" — check it still plays.
- Shop title is "wool knitting boutique." That's what Google shows; "Handmade alpaca & merino knitwear — scarves, sweaters, wraps" works harder.
- Social media sent 22 visits in two years and Share & Save shows no activity. Pinning every listing with the wuulo.etsy.com link saves the 4% fee on anything it brings in; knitwear does well on Pinterest.
- Reply to each of the five reviews (shoppers see that), and turn on the post-purchase thank-you offer — there is exactly 1 repeat buyer.

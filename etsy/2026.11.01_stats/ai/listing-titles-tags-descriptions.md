## How these are built

Every title below follows Etsy's own guidance from April 2026: under 15 words, the item named first, then the traits that decide a purchase — material, pattern or neckline, fit, colours, who it's for. Words like *luxury*, *artisanal*, *handcrafted* and *winter gift* are gone from the titles: Etsy says they belong in tags and descriptions, and they were occupying the first, most heavily weighted words of every current title. Source: [New Guidance for Listing Titles, and a Tool to Help](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1399426136697) (Etsy Seller Handbook, 27 Apr 2026).

- **Title pattern:** item · material · pattern/neckline · fit · colours · who it's for. Example: *Merino wool tweed sweater, mock neck, regular fit, petrol, red or terracotta, unisex.*
- **"Throw scarf", "shawl scarf" and "throw" are retired.** A 40 × 190 cm piece is a scarf or a wrap — the words buyers type. Every title now says *unisex* or *for women*; none of the current 18 say who the piece is for.
- **Tags:** all 13 slots, each a 2–3 word phrase under 20 characters: the main term, its synonyms (wrap, jumper, poncho), colours, and the wearer. Colour and material also go in the attribute fields, which Etsy counts as search terms too.
- **Descriptions:** the first two sentences say what it is, who it's for and how it feels — that is all Google and Etsy's preview show. Then a short spec list (fibre, stitch, size, colours), then the shared blocks from the next section. Your yarn counts, gauge, model measurements and product passport stay in: they are real differentiators.
- **Per listing you get:** current title → new title (word count), 13 tags, the unique part of the description, and a one-line fix for anything wrong on that listing (photos, duplicated listings, conflicting measurements).
- Etsy now offers an AI title-suggestion tool in the search visibility dashboard. Comparing its suggestions with these is fine, but it only sees the current title, first photo and description.

## Shared blocks for every listing

Paste these four blocks at the end of every description, in this order, after the listing's own text. They replace the shipping, care and packaging paragraphs already in your listings and add the two things buyers currently can't find: a lead time and the 30-day return window. The one fact in square brackets is missing from every listing — fill it once.

**Sizing & fit** *(sweaters, vests and tops only)*

Measurements are taken with the piece laid flat: width across the sleeves and height without the collar, as in the size list above. Our model is 167 cm (bust 82, waist 60, hips 95 cm) and wears size S unless the listing says otherwise. Between sizes, or want it longer, shorter or in another colour? Send us a message — custom sizes and colours cost nothing extra.

**Shipping & returns**

Made in our Kyiv workshop and shipped worldwide with Ukrposhta, fully tracked — you get the tracking number by email the day it ships. Delivery is included in the price. US buyers: we prepay import duties, so there is nothing to pay on delivery. UK and EU buyers: VAT is collected by Etsy at checkout. [Ready-to-ship pieces leave within X working days; made-to-order pieces within X days.] Returns and exchanges accepted within 30 days; the buyer covers return postage.

**Care**

Wool needs washing far less often than you think — air it between wears and wash only when it really needs it. Hand wash cold with a wool-safe detergent, don't soak or wring, then dry and store flat. A delicate cold wool cycle in the machine also works. Light pilling is natural with soft fibres like alpaca and mohair; a lint remover takes it off in seconds.

**Packaging & gifting**

Each piece comes in a cardboard box with its product passport: the story of the yarn, the stitch and the hands that made it. The labels and the passport are printed on seed paper — plant them and they grow into wildflowers. Sending it as a gift? We'll wrap the box in a knitted ribbon, add a card with your message and email you a photo before it ships, at no extra charge.

## Shop-level fixes to do alongside

Do these in the same sitting as the titles — several of them undo the benefit of a good title if left as they are.

- [ ] **Rename the sections:** "wool scarfs and throws" → *Scarves & wraps*; "wool sweaters" → *Sweaters & tops*.
- [ ] **Merge the duplicates:** listings 5 & 6 (batwing sweater) into 4485196479; decide on listings 10 & 17 (the 40 × 220 cm baby alpaca scarf at $76 and $108).
- [ ] **Free shipping on the six scarves under $100** (listings 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 16): they show no free-shipping badge while every other listing does, and your FAQ already says delivery is included. Etsy's US search favours free-shipping listings, so these six are being held back for nothing.
- [ ] **Fix the three listings with two sizes on one page:** 12 (40 × 190 vs 45 × 215), 14 (210 vs 200 cm), 18 (45 × 185 vs 36 × 200). Keep the measured one in both the description and the highlights.
- [ ] **Materials field:** 12 says *alpaca, wool* against a 100% royal alpaca text; 15 has no percentages at all. Only listing 14 states fibre percentages — add them everywhere you know them.
- [ ] **Missing colour options:** listing 3 (tan, brown, mustard in photos) and 16 (two colourways in photos) have no colour variation, so buyers can't choose.
- [ ] **Sold-out variants:** across the nine garments most S and L sizes are greyed out, and several scarves show every colour as sold out next to a "3 left" notice. Since you make to order anyway, set quantity on every size and colour and state the lead time in the shipping block.
- [ ] **Processing time:** it's missing from every listing, and it's what Etsy builds the delivery estimate from. Set it in the shipping profiles and fill the bracket in the shared block.
- [ ] **Attributes on every listing:** primary and secondary colour, material, recipient (women / men / unisex), occasion, style, and neckline for the garments. These count as search terms, so the tags above don't repeat them.
- [ ] **Categories:** scarves under *Accessories › Scarves & Wraps › Scarves*; sweaters and vests under *Clothing › Gender-Neutral Adult Clothing › Sweaters* unless you decide a piece is women's only.
- [ ] **Back up first, then wait:** download the listings CSV before editing (Etsy's own advice), make the changes in one sitting, and give search a few weeks before judging — Etsy says title changes take time to take effect.

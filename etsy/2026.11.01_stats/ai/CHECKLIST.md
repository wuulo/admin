**The routine for every listing** (10–15 min). Open `paste/<file>.md` next to the listing editor.

1. **Photos.** Drag the photo named in the table below to position 1. Where no worn shot exists, the first photo is the one that shows the **whole piece** on a hanger or laid flat, never a folded stack or a texture close-up; close-ups go third or later. No new photos.
2. **Variations.** Give every size and colour a quantity (you make to order anyway), so nothing shows "sold out". Add a colour option where the table says one is missing. If the listing is now mostly made-to-order, move it to the 3–5 day shipping profile so the delivery estimate stays honest.
3. **Title.** Paste section 1 of the file.
4. **Tags.** Remove all 13 existing tags, then paste section 2 (the whole comma-separated line) into the tag box; Etsy splits it into 13 tags.
5. **Description.** Select everything in the description box and paste section 3. Listing 9 in the order (4473253283) still has a `[colour]` bracket to fill from D5; every other file pastes as is.
6. **Attributes and category.** Primary and secondary colour, material, recipient (unisex adults / women, per D2), occasion, style, neckline for garments. Category: scarves under *Accessories › Scarves & Wraps › Scarves*; sweaters and vests under *Clothing › Gender-Neutral Adult Clothing › Sweaters* (or Women's, per D2).
7. **The fix.** Do what section 4 of the file says (a measurement to settle, a materials field to correct, a listing to merge). Save. Open the public page and read it once as a buyer.

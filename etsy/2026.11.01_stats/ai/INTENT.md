# Intent — wuulo Etsy sales plan, Nov 2026 – Jan 2027

*Agreed with Flavia Goga on 1 Nov 2026 in a Claude Code interview (VS Code), after the review in `shop-review-2026-10-31.md`. This is the "why" and the bar; the "what to do" is `CHECKLIST.md`.*

- **Outcome:** A checklist Flavia works through in Shop Manager that turns the 18 listings into ones that convert and get found, in that order: listing fixes first, then titles and tags, then a small ads top-up.
- **User:** Flavia, alone, a few hours a week. She does the clicking in Shop Manager.
- **Why now:** Gift season runs November to January. The shop has sold 13 pieces in two years. The rewrite in `listing-titles-tags-descriptions.md` is drafted and nothing is applied yet.
- **Success:** 15 to 20 orders between 1 Nov 2026 and 31 Jan 2027, conversion above 1%, from search or ads. Either channel counts.
- **Constraint:** No photos on a person for now. Every photo fix uses the images already uploaded (reorder, pick a different first image). Sold-out sizes and colours become made-to-order with a stated lead time.
- **Out of scope:** New listings (beanies, cowls, colourway splits), a model photo shoot, the Etsy Open API, the website, Instagram and Pinterest. Ads stay at $3 to 5 a day on the proven listings and are not scaled.

## Decisions taken during the interview

- The review's priority order stands: conversion first, visibility second. "Organic search" was the label; sales are the goal.
- Checklist, not automation. Etsy's listings CSV is export-only, and the API needs an app key and review; 18 listings do not justify that.

## How to judge it

Re-export Shop Stats and the ads CSV around 1 Dec 2026 and 1 Jan 2027, re-run `etsy-ads-analysis.py`, and compare orders, conversion and search visits against the all-time baseline in `HANDOVER.md` §7 (0.4% conversion, 195 search visits all time).
